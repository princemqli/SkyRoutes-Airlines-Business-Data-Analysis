BUSINESS INSIGHTS

-   Top 10 flight routes contribute the highest share of overall profit.
-   Revenue and operational costs vary across different routes,
    highlighting the importance of cost management.
-   Monthly profit trends indicate seasonal fluctuations in airline
    demand and business performance.
-   Higher seat occupancy generally results in better operational
    efficiency and increased profitability.
-   International routes generate higher revenue compared to most
    domestic routes.
-   The dashboard enables management to monitor performance, identify
    profitable routes, and support data-driven business decisions.
