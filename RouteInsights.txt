ROUTE INSIGHTS

-   The analysis identifies the most profitable flight routes based on
    total revenue and profit.
-   Some routes generate high revenue but also have high operational
    costs, reducing overall profit.
-   Monthly profit analysis helps identify seasonal demand patterns and
    peak business periods.
-   Aircraft utilisation and seat occupancy directly impact route
    profitability.
-   Route-wise analysis helps management optimise flight operations and
    improve resource allocation.
-   Interactive filters allow detailed analysis by route, aircraft type,
    and travel month.
